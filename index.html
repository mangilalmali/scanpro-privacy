Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Welcome to SCAN PRO AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

SCAN PRO AI does not require users to create an account or log in. The application is designed to process documents locally on your device.

We do not intentionally collect personal information through document scanning or PDF creation.

2. Document Processing

Document scanning, OCR (Optical Character Recognition), and PDF creation are performed locally on your device, provided the relevant features are configured to work offline.

Your scanned documents are not intentionally uploaded to our servers.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share your scanned documents or personal information with third parties.

If third-party SDKs, advertising services, or analytics tools are integrated into the application, their data collection and sharing practices will be disclosed where applicable.

4. Storage and Deletion

Documents and PDF files created using SCAN PRO AI are stored locally on your device, depending on your chosen save location.

You can delete saved documents using the application or your device's file manager.

Documents may remain in device backups or other locations depending on your device settings.

5. Permissions

SCAN PRO AI may request the following permissions depending on the features you use:

  • Camera permission for scanning physical documents.
  • Photo, media, or file access for importing and saving documents.

These permissions are used to provide the relevant application features.

6. Data Security

We rely on your device's operating system and local storage protections to help protect your documents. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and saved files.

7. Children's Privacy

SCAN PRO AI is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
